Privacy & Safety

Protection without surveillance

The commitments SafeHer is engineered around.

Location is consent-based

Nothing is shared by default. You pick each person and choose always-on, temporary, paused or stopped sharing. Stopping a share removes your last position from it. The web app only reads location while open and after you grant browser permission.

Microphone is never used covertly

The web app uses the microphone only for a visible, user-started test. The future Android app will run voice detection on-device with a persistent notification, and raw audio is never uploaded.

Who can see an emergency

Only you, and the approved contacts who were actually alerted for that emergency. Contacts keep access for 24 hours after it's resolved or cancelled, then lose it automatically. Blocking or removing a contact ends their access immediately. Administrators see redacted activity only (no location, notes or contact details). Security-relevant actions such as invitations, approvals, role changes and emergency actions are audit-logged. Logging of every read of sensitive data is planned but not yet in place.

You approve who joins your circle

When someone accepts your invitation, you see the name, email and phone of the account that accepted, and whether it matches who you invited. Nobody receives your alerts or location until you approve them, so a forwarded invitation link can't silently grant access.

Community helpers

Helpers are opted-in community members whose identity has been checked. They are not police officers or professional responders. They receive only an approximate distance, never your exact location.

Honest status, no false promises

We show Sent, Delivered or Acknowledged only once the relevant system confirms it. SafeHer depends on networks and devices, so alert delivery can never be guaranteed. Always call 112 in immediate danger.

Your data, your control

Invitations are single-use and expire. You can block or remove contacts, leave someone's circle, and permanently delete your account and all of your data from Account Settings.

Security

HTTPS everywhere, Firebase Authentication, server-side authorization on every API call, input validation, rate limiting, Firestore Security Rules that deny by default, and no secrets in client code.

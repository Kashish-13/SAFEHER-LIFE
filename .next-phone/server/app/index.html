How SafeHer responds

In an emergency, you shouldn't have to call five people.

  1. 1

    Trigger once

    Press SOS, or (in the native app) say your phrase. A cancel window guards against accidents.

  2. 2

    Your circle is alerted together

    Every primary contact gets your alert, time and authorised location simultaneously.

  3. 3

    Escalates if nobody responds

    No acknowledgement in time? Secondary contacts are alerted, exactly once. In this version the check runs while your emergency screen is open; fully server-side scheduling is in development.

  4. 4

    Coordinate until you're safe

    See who has acknowledged, share live location, call 112, then mark yourself safe.

All features

Built on consent, not surveillance.

Your location is shared only with people you choose, only when you choose, and you can revoke it instantly. Helpers never see your exact position. Admins never see your location.

Our privacy commitments
  • No background microphone or location monitoring without explicit opt-in
  • Voice detection runs on-device in the native app; raw audio is never uploaded
  • Delivery status is shown only after the system confirms it
  • SafeHer is not police dispatch. Call 112 is always one tap away
  • Delete your account and data at any time

How it works

From one tap to a coordinated response

  1. 0s

    You trigger SOS

    Press and confirm the SOS button. A configurable countdown (default 5 seconds) lets you cancel an accidental press before anything is sent.

  2. 1s

    An emergency event is created

    SafeHer records the time, your trigger type and, if you've allowed it, your latest location from your device.

  3. 1s

    Primary contacts are alerted simultaneously

    Every accepted primary contact receives an in-app alert (and a push notification where configured) at the same moment. No one-by-one calling.

  4. live

    You see real delivery status

    Each contact moves through Sent → Delivered → Acknowledged, and each step appears only once the relevant system confirms it.

  5. 2 min

    Escalation if nobody acknowledges

    If no primary contact acknowledges within your configured window, secondary contacts are alerted, exactly once. Current limitation: this check runs while your emergency screen is open (with a once-a-day server backstop). A server-side scheduler that works with every screen closed is in development. Until then, keep the emergency screen open if you can, and call 112 in immediate danger.

  6. end

    You mark yourself safe

    Resolve or cancel the emergency. Everyone alerted is told immediately, and the event is saved to your private history.

About voice-activated SOS

Web browsers cannot listen to the microphone when a tab is closed or the phone is locked, so locked-screen voice detection will ship in the native Android app. It uses on-device keyword spotting in a foreground service with a visible notification, and never uploads raw audio. In the web app you can configure your phrase, test your microphone while the page is open, and run a clearly labelled simulated trigger. Even on Android, OS battery restrictions mean background detection can never be guaranteed, so manual SOS always remains available.

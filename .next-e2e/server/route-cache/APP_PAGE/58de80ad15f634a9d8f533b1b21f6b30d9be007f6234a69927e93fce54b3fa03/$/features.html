Features

A complete personal safety toolkit

Built around one idea: a single trigger should start a coordinated response.

Available

One-tap SOS

A large, unmistakable SOS button with a short cancel window. One trigger alerts your whole circle at the same time.

Available

Trusted Emergency Circle

Invite family and friends, set primary and secondary contacts, organise circles, and see who has accepted.

Coming next

Smart escalation

If no primary contact acknowledges in time, secondary contacts are alerted, exactly once. Currently runs while your emergency screen is open; server-side scheduling is coming next.

Available

Honest delivery tracking

See sent, delivered, acknowledged, declined and failed per contact. Never shown before the system confirms it.

Available

Family location sharing

Choose exactly who sees your location: always, for a set time, paused or stopped. Revoke at any moment.

Available

Safe Journey Guardian

Share your trip, check in along the way. Missed check-ins remind you first, then escalate only if you opted in.

Native app

Voice-activated SOS

Say your personal phrase to trigger SOS. Configure and test it here; locked-screen detection requires the native Android app.

Available

Emergency services

One tap to dial 112 and India's women's helplines, plus nearby hospitals and police stations on the map.

Available

Fake call

Schedule a realistic simulated incoming call to excuse yourself from an uncomfortable situation.

Coming next

Verified helper network

Request help from nearby, opted-in, identity-verified community members, who only ever see your approximate distance.

Coming next

Group voice room

Bring everyone who responds into one in-app voice room to coordinate, with no chain of phone calls.

Coming next

Secure evidence vault

User-initiated recordings, photos and notes, stored privately with timestamps and access control.

SafeHer

Your Safety. Your Circle. Your Protection. A coordinated emergency response platform built around consent and privacy.

SafeHer is not an emergency service and is not integrated with police dispatch. Alerts depend on network connectivity and device settings and cannot be guaranteed. In immediate danger, call 112.
